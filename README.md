# Neueface

**The infinite canvas for your React app.** A Mac app that puts your real components and pages on
one canvas, live, side by side. Explore, build and edit interfaces with your coding agent, right in
your codebase.

[neueface.com](https://neueface.com)

## Download

- **[neueface.com/download](https://neueface.com/download)** gives you the newest version your
  license covers. Every version is also under [Releases](https://github.com/Neueface/releases/releases).
- Neueface needs a license: [neueface.com/pricing](https://neueface.com/pricing).
- Requires a Mac with Apple Silicon (M1 or later), macOS 13 or newer, and Node.js 22+.

## Updates

The app updates itself: when a new version is out, an Update button shows in the tab bar, and one
click installs it. What changed is in each [release](https://github.com/Neueface/releases/releases).
